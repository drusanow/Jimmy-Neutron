Connect your Neutron

Plug the Neutron into this computer with USB and press Connect. The browser asks for permission to use MIDI devices with system exclusive messages, allow it.

Settings need the neutr_cube firmware on the Neutron. With Behringer's firmware, use the Firmware tab to install it.

Automatic tuning

The Neutron measures its oscillators and calculates the calibration. Let it warm up for 30 minutes first, and do not play while it runs (a few seconds per oscillator). You will hear it.

Tuner

Listens through the computer's microphone (or an audio input connected to the Neutron's output).

Test notes

Click a note to start it, click again to stop. Notes go out on the Neutron's MIDI channel.

Manual calibration

  1. Note scale. Play C2 and C5. Change Note DAC scale until both are equally in tune.
  2. Octave scale. Hold a note and switch Range. Change Octave DAC scale until the octaves are clean.
  3. Pitch offset. Play A4. Change Pitch offset until the tuner shows 0 cents.

Changes are heard immediately. Press Save to Neutron to keep them.

Update or restore firmware

The Neutron's chip has a USB bootloader built into ROM. It can not be erased, so a failed update can always be repeated. Installing neutr_cube keeps your saved settings and calibration. Installing a full 128 kB image (such as Behringer's) replaces them, recalibrate afterwards.
